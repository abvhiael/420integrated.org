Applications / Compute

420 AI.

420 AI is the ecosystem interface for distributed AI work: requesting jobs, routing them to qualified compute providers and settling completed work through network-native infrastructure.

what happens on-chain

Registries, job routing, escrow, provider staking and service-level evidence can be anchored on-chain while model execution remains off-chain on suitable compute hardware.

provider roles

Compute providers are expected to advertise capabilities, accept work under defined terms and produce the evidence required by the job or protocol. Consumers should be able to inspect job state and payment settlement without trusting a single operator.

payments & accountability

Jobs are intended to settle in the native ecosystem economy with staking, slashing and SLA-related protections available where the protocol requires them.

Production provider requirements, endpoint formats, job schemas and pricing policy will be documented after the corresponding 420 AI services complete qualification.
