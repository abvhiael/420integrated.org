Applications / Identity, Registry & Names

shared identity & discovery.

420 Identity, 420 Registry and 420 Names are designed to provide reusable identity, canonical records and human-readable discovery across the ecosystem.

420 Identity

Identity services provide reusable account-linked identity and authorization primitives without requiring every application to invent its own identity model.

420 Registry

Registry records are intended to provide canonical references for ecosystem resources such as applications, contracts, services and other verifiable records.

420 Names

Names add human-readable identifiers that can resolve to network resources while remaining anchored to canonical records and ownership rules.

why they work together

Applications can use these shared services to reduce duplicated account systems, ambiguous addresses and unofficial references.

Final schemas, record types and deployment addresses will be published as the genesis configuration is qualified.
